WDAY — Workday Inc. | Long, 3% starter | 5-10yr hold

Price $129.34 | Mkt Cap $33.24B | EV $31.62B | FY26 Rev $9.55B | FY26 FCF $2.78B JAOD, May 19, 2026.

Thesis (bumper-sticker): Market prices a third outcome no model supports — Workday loses the AI franchise AND fails to pivot to cash. The cash math works in both real branches; $129 is a five-year low against a base that grew revenue 13% and AI ARR from ~zero to $400M+ in one year.

Why we own it

  • Contracts are the floor. $28.1B subscription backlog (~3yrs revenue), 97% gross retention, 65% of F500 wired in. Even in What Goes Wrong (rev −7%/yr), FY30 FCF/sh holds at $10.30 and share count drops 268M → 215M.
  • AI is landing, not failing. $400M+ AI ARR (+100% YoY), 12 agents at GA with 400+ customers, 1.7B AI actions FY26, Sana acquired and shipped to GA in 4 months (fastest in company history).
  • Bhusri's return is a Good-AI signal, not a regression. Board paid $1.25M base + 200% bonus + large equity to bring the founder back. Boards do that when the bet is strategic, not when the operator playbook needs tightening. GTM team intact (Enslin/Rowe/Kazmaier).
  • Integration economics flip in Workday's favor. PS revenue declined nominally ($728M → $719M) while customers grew 8% — AI code-gen drops 12-18mo deployments to ~6mo, opening the 10K-EE mid-enterprise tier that couldn't justify the prior $20M cutover cost.

What would kill the trade

  • Gross revenue retention sustains below 95% for two consecutive quarters (today 97%) — the disruption signal.
  • Microsoft acquires Dayforce/ADP or ships a Copilot HCM SKU under $25/seat. Threat is 15-20%, not 10%. Self-Service Agent (May 13, 2026) is the front-door tell.
  • AI ARR growth decelerates below 50% YoY for two quarters (today >100%).
  • FAD 2026 passes with no sales-force comp redesign. Without it, Flex Credits adoption stalls at 3-5% of new bookings by FY28 and the per-seat repricing risk compounds.

Timeline

  • May 21, 2026 — Q1 FY27 print (first Bhusri quarter). Bull tell: AI ARR run-rate >$500M. Bear tell: sub growth <12%.
  • Aug 2026 — Q2 FY27 print. Watch AI ARR >$600M and NRR commentary tone.
  • Sept 2026 — FAD 2026 (Bhusri's first as returned CEO). Binary: sales-comp redesign for consumption pricing yes/no. The single most diagnostic event on the calendar.
  • Feb 2027 — Q4 FY27 print, first full year with Agent SoR live. Watch gross retention and headcount-clause repricing flags in MD&A.
