Workday (WDAY) at $129 — long, 3% starter, 5-10 year hold

JAOD, May 20, 2026.

The thesis in one box. Great software for the biggest, most complex businesses in the world. Clunkiness solved by AI. Competitive threats are real but slow-moving — we will know whether they have landed in two to three years. From here, the company goes in one of two directions, both of which pay shareholders: (1) a truly magical AI-powered product that is unique and not replicable, or (2) running itself for cash. The market prices neither outcome correctly today.

A franchise the market valued at $80 billion two years ago is on offer at $33 billion. Workday runs payroll for roughly half of corporate America. System of record for HR and Financials at more than 65% of the Fortune 500. 75 million employees, 11,500 customers, $9.55B of revenue. Five-year low at $129. Down 47% in 18 months while the S&P is up roughly 30% over the same window.

Three scenarios on a four-year horizon. The market prices a single set of metrics for both outcomes; the two real outcomes deserve two scoreboards.
