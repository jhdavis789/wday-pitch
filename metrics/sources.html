How to read growth

Long-term growth is the three-year compound annual growth rate (CAGR), measured between the same fiscal quarter or annual period where both endpoints exist. A two-year CAGR is labeled explicitly when a three-year endpoint is unavailable. No annualized rate is shown for shorter history. Quarter, annual, year-to-date, trailing-twelve-month and point-in-time observations remain separate.

Latest growth compares each observation with the same period one year earlier: (current ÷ prior − 1) × 100. Acceleration is latest year-on-year growth minus the year-on-year rate one year earlier, in percentage points. This compares two annual growth rates; it is not sequential-quarter growth. Percentage metrics use percentage-point changes, and their long-term column shows annualized percentage-point change. Percent growth is not shown from a zero or negative base, or to a negative endpoint. Targets, irregular event periods and open-ended thresholds do not receive exact growth rates.

Teal means the growth rate increased; magenta means it decreased. Neither color is an investment recommendation. Dots are actual disclosed observations; gaps are not interpolated. Dollar amounts are displayed in millions where the source data are in dollars. Every value retains its own source link.

Period and measurement conventions

Workday’s fiscal year ends January 31. Q2 FY2027 ended July 31, 2026. The main window is July 2023 through July 2026, with FY2023 and early-2023 comparison anchors retained where available. Later comparable disclosures are used where they revise an earlier figure. Retention rates, product attach rates and penetration figures preserve their disclosed denominators. Threshold customer counts are labeled and do not imply exact growth. Product definitions and acquisition scope can change; observation notes identify known comparability limits.

Known coverage limits

  • Prepared remarks for Q2 FY24 through Q1 FY25 not located in primary-source archive.
  • Q&A portion of earnings calls not transcribed from primary webcast.
  • No assumed three-year history for newly introduced agents or ad hoc operating disclosures.
  • Product attach rates have 2023, 2024 and 2025 July snapshots; 2026 analyst day had not yet occurred at October 4 cutoff.
  • Reported threshold observations do not support precise growth rates.
  • Historical customer satisfaction population changed; organic-agent definition/availability broadened.
  • July 2023 12-month backlog is not shown in the contemporary release (which used 24-month backlog); filing data may supply retrospective comparison. Do not substitute 24-month backlog for cRPO.
  • This is a broad inventory of investment-relevant recurring metrics and selected one-off disclosures, not literally every numeral (e.g. slide numbers, executive tenure, illustrative hypothetical contracts).
  • Main annual subscription and margin guidance from August 2023 onward included; detailed services, OCF, capex and next-quarter guidance before Q2 FY2025 are not exhaustively transcribed.
  • Some sporadic operating metrics have one observation and no disclosed three-year comparable series. These must display insufficient history rather than invented growth.
  • 2026 analyst day scheduled October 13, after October 4 cutoff, so no 2026 analyst-day figures included.
  • TAM methodology/product coverage evolves. Market estimates should not be treated as booked revenue or direct business growth.
  • Selected Q&A disclosures are provided separately from secondary verbatim transcripts. Coverage ledger lists exact sources; this is not a completeness certification.
  • Primary audio replay was not transcribed independently. Secondary Q&A sources are labeled.
  • Bank-conference Q&A and analyst-day spoken Q&A beyond slides not exhaustively reviewed.
  • Illustrative customer-level ROI metrics do not establish Workday-wide performance; bases, sample sizes and validation are often undisclosed.
  • Numbers from analyst questions were excluded unless confirmed by management.
  • Repeated financial and AI figures already captured from primary sources excluded to avoid duplicates.
  • Q2 FY27 secondary transcript contains an erroneous subscription revenue amount and conflicting internal-agent counts. Official filing/remarks used instead; conflicting Q&A count excluded.
