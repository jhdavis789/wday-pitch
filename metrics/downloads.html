Choose the reporting period

Filter frequency to quarter for standalone quarterly observations or annual for full fiscal years. ytd contains cumulative periods and must be kept separate. instant contains point-in-time balances and snapshots. Some operating KPIs are quarterly snapshots rather than three-month flows; a blank start date does not establish a flow interval.

Annual and quarterly rows are included where disclosed or defensibly derived. Missing history is not estimated. A forecast is identified by observation_kind; an acquisition, product, instrument or geography slice is identified by dimensions_json and the metric definition. Do not sum overlapping slices.

Read values and growth

The CSV has one observation per row and repeats each metric description so rows remain self-contained. Parse it with a CSV library: descriptions and notes can contain commas and quoted text. Values are numeric in the stated unit; a percent level of 31.1 means 31.1%. Empty numeric fields mean unavailable, not zero. Preserve qualifiers such as ≥ and ≈.

Growth columns distinguish percent growth from percentage-point change through growth_unit. long_term_basis identifies the horizon and calculation. Derived quarters retain primary and comparison source links. No new quarterly EPS or margin is manufactured by subtracting annual amounts.
